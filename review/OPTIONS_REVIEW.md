# Hearing-aid options review

User approved Layout A + headline 1: “Hearing aids chosen around your life.” Implemented for rendered review, not locked.

## Implementation

An introduction/product row is followed by three full-width benefit columns and a compact manufacturer/style-guide row. Mobile begins with the headline before the image, then full-width stacked benefit passages. Tinted background, restrained icons and rules create a different rhythm from the white process section. Body and qualifications remain 18px. No extra CTA interrupts the existing conversion path.

All eight manufacturers and the complete original style guide are preserved. Copy retains the hearing-needs/lifestyle/budget basis, conditional suitability, small-style limitations, custom impressions when needed, personalized settings, pairing/streaming/environment presets and model/phone compatibility. No guaranteed outcomes or universal invisibility are promised.

## Image limitation

A real in-hand photograph remains the preferred final asset. None was supplied or available locally. The research-recorded clinic Widex SmartRIC URL is inserted as a labelled model example at native resolution rather than enlarged. Local network retrieval is blocked (403); the screenshots show its native missing-image/alt rendering, not a verified product rendering. Check the real image in GHL and ideally replace with a sharper authentic in-hand photo. No generated device, invented asset URL, or unrelated image is used.
